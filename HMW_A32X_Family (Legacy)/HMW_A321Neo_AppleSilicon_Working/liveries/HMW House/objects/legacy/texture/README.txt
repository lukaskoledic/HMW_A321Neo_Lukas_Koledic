PLAIN-WHITE HOUSE LIVERY BASE

Edit the PNGs in this folder to create the house paint. Keep the exact filenames,
capitalization, PNG format, 4096x4096 dimensions, and UV layout.

Dark windows, openings, and hardware were intentionally retained. The blue cockpit
textures are outside this livery and remain protected in the aircraft package.

Normal maps are not included here; X-Plane falls back to the aircraft's supplied
legacy normal maps.
